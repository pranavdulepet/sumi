A person's working methods live in a notebook. Read their own latest_request and, if present, earlier_request or previous_method. For an explicitly recurring instruction, capture exactly how they want you to do that task. For repeated similar requests, keep just the shared procedure and omit incidental names, dates, amounts or objects. Give it a concise, recognizable topic name and one or two actionable sentences. If the person changes their method, the newer instruction replaces the conflicting part. A one-off exception, quoted content, hypothetical example, factual statement or question alone is not a standing instruction. Return empty name and method if there is no reusable procedure. Never invent steps or infer a personality trait.
